# AI Response Evaluation Rubric

## 1. Factual Accuracy

5 — Claims are accurate and adequately supported.
4 — Substantially accurate, with a minor issue.
3 — Partially accurate, with a meaningful omission or error.
2 — Contains significant factual errors.
1 — Fundamentally incorrect or seriously misleading.

## 2. Instruction Adherence

5 — Follows all material instructions.
4 — Follows the instructions with a minor omission.
3 — Satisfies some requirements but misses an important one.
2 — Fails to follow several important requirements.
1 — Does not meaningfully address the requested task.

## 3. Relevance

5 — Directly addresses the user's actual need.
4 — Mostly relevant, with minor unnecessary content.
3 — Partially relevant.
2 — Mostly irrelevant or poorly targeted.
1 — Does not address the question.

## 4. Reasoning Quality

5 — Conclusions follow logically from the evidence.
4 — Reasoning is sound, with a minor weakness.
3 — Some conclusions lack adequate justification.
2 — Contains major logical gaps.
1 — Reasoning is fundamentally unsound.

## 5. Clarity

5 — Clear, precise, and easy to understand.
4 — Understandable, with minor ambiguity.
3 — Some confusing or unnecessarily complex sections.
2 — Difficult to follow.
1 — Largely incomprehensible.

## Required Evaluation Notes

For every response, record:

- The criterion being assessed.
- The score.
- The specific evidence supporting the score.
- The impact of any identified weakness.
- A recommended correction where appropriate.

## Important Rules

- Do not infer correctness from confident language.
- Distinguish factual errors from stylistic preferences.
- Do not penalize a response for omitting information
  that the user did not need.
- Verify important factual claims using reliable sources.
- Mark uncertainty when the available evidence is insufficient.
- Apply the same standards across comparable responses.
